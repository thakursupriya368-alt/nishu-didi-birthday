I don't think I tell you this enough, and honestly, I don't even know how to say all of it properly. But I love you so, so much.

I want you to be happy. I want you to achieve everything you're working for. I want life to be kind to you, too. You spend so much time doing things for other people, and I hope you always make room for yourself and the things that make YOU happy.

No matter how far away you are, you'll always be my Nishu. And no matter how grown-up life gets, you'll always have your little sibling cheering for you from here.

I love you. I miss you. I'm proud of you. And I hope you never have to wonder whether you mean a lot to me, because you really, really do. 💗
